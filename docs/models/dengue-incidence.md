## What is forecast

Weekly reported **dengue incidence** (cases per 100,000 population; WHO Global Dengue Surveillance and World Bank population) for 49 weekly-reporting countries in dengue coverage tiers A and B, 1, 2, 4 weeks after the latest settled week. One pooled model per horizon (direct strategy), trained on log(incidence + one case per 100,000 population), with tree models learning the change from this week. Case definitions differ between countries (suspected, probable or confirmed), so a forecast describes a country's own reporting, not comparable infection rates. Errors below are in cases per 100,000 per week.

## Backtest

All models are scored on the same country-weeks. Errors in cases per 100,000 per week. Intervals: sequential split conformal (normalised by 8-week volatility, calibrated per case-count group on earlier origins only; first 6 origins have none).

## Does weather help?

Each tree model is fitted twice on the same rows and origins: with the base features only, and with 18 ERA5 weather features added (4-week mean temperature, total precipitation and mean humidity ending 4, 8 and 12 weeks before the origin, and their anomalies from the country's 2013–2022 normal). Change = MAE with weather ÷ MAE without − 1 (negative = weather lowers the error). 95% interval: bootstrap over origins (2000 resamples). A weather model is used only if it wins the backtest selection above.

## Data and limitations

- Training targets from 2014-09-29; provisional weeks are excluded. COVID-19 weeks are kept (dengue transmission and reporting continued).
- Half of the modelled countries report weekly to WHO only from 2023–2024, so their history is short; seasonal-naive needs a year of history and rows without it are not scored.
- Weekly reporters without a World Bank population (Guadeloupe, Martinique, Wallis and Futuna) have no incidence and are not modelled; monthly reporters are not forecast (their months are not split into invented weeks).
- The backtest uses today's (revised) data, not data as first published; recent dengue weeks are often revised upwards, so real-time accuracy can be lower.
- Weather is ERA5 at one point per country (docs/datasets/weather.md); large or climatically varied countries are poorly represented by it.
- Neighbour features use spatial neighbours among the modelled countries; island areas without a boundary have none.
