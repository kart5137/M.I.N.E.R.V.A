## Backtest

Rolling origin, expanding window: origins every 2 weeks from 2024-10-07 to the reference week minus the horizon; ML models refitted every 8 weeks on targets known at the refit origin. All models are scored on the same country-weeks. Errors in percentage points (pp) of positivity. Intervals: sequential split conformal (normalised by 8-week volatility, calibrated per testing-volume group on earlier origins only; first 6 origins have none).

## Data and limitations

- Training targets from 2014-09-29; target weeks in the COVID-19 window (2020-03-01 → 2022-06-30), provisional weeks and weeks with fewer than 10 specimens are excluded.
- The backtest uses today's (revised) data, not data as first published, so real-time accuracy can be lower, especially at the 1-week horizon.
- Positivity depends on who is tested; changes in testing practice shift it.
- Neighbour-based spatial features need the spatial weights built in M6; only the transmission-zone average is used now.
- Forecasts are produced only for countries with a valid positivity in the reference week.
